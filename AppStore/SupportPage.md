## How to install Bela Keyboard

1. Download Bela Keyboard from the App Store.
2. Open **Settings** → **General** → **Keyboard** → **Keyboards** → **Add New Keyboard…**
3. Tap **Bela Keyboard**.
4. Open any app (Messages, Notes, etc.), tap the input field, then tap and hold the 🌐 globe on the system keyboard.
5. Choose **Bela Keyboard**.

You're ready to type in Ge'ez script!

---

## How to type Tigrinya

- Tap a base consonant (e.g. ህ, ለ, ሐ) to see its **vowel popup** — the 7 vowel forms appear above the key.
- Tap any vowel form to insert it.
- Tap **123** for numbers and Ethiopic punctuation.
- Tap **ABC** for Latin letters.
- Tap **😀** for emoji.
- Tap **ግዕዝ** to return to the Ge'ez grid.
- Tap the 🌐 globe to cycle between keyboards.
- The **language pill** (ትግ / አማ) cycles between Tigrinya, Tigre, and Amharic word suggestions.

## Frequently asked questions

### My keyboard does not show after install. What do I do?
Make sure you've added it under **Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards**. Then, in any text field, **press and hold** the 🌐 globe icon on the system keyboard and pick **Bela Keyboard**.

### Why are there no haptics when I type?
By default Bela Keyboard does not request **"Allow Full Access"**, which is what iOS requires for haptic feedback in third-party keyboards. You can enable it in **Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Bela Keyboard → Allow Full Access**. We perform no network requests even with Full Access enabled.

### How do I unlock the premium themes?
Open the Bela Keyboard app, scroll to the **Premium** section, and tap **Unlock Premium Themes — $2.99**. The purchase is one-time and applies on all your devices signed in to the same Apple ID.

### I bought premium themes on another device. How do I restore?
Open the Bela Keyboard app → **Premium** section → tap **Restore Purchases**.

### Does Bela Keyboard collect my typing or send it anywhere?
**No.** Your keystrokes never leave your device. See our [Privacy Policy](./PrivacyPolicy.md) for details.

### Will Bela Keyboard ever add voice typing?
That's planned for a future version, using on-device speech recognition so your audio also never leaves your device.

### What languages are supported?
- **Tigrinya** (ትግርኛ) — primary
- **Tigre** (ትግረ)
- **Amharic** (አማርኛ)

All three share the Ge'ez script. The language picker mainly controls which word list is used for typing suggestions.
