# Privacy Policy — Bela ቤላ Keyboard

**Effective date:** May 30, 2026
**Developer:** Antonyo S MICHAEL
**Contact:** support@belakeyboard.app

This is the privacy policy for **Bela Keyboard** (the "App"), a Tigrinya/Ge'ez keyboard for iPhone, available on the Apple App Store.

We've designed Bela Keyboard to be **as private as possible**. Your typing is yours. Read on for the details.

---

## 1. Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

- The keystrokes you type
- The words or text you produce
- Your contacts, photos, location, or any device identifiers
- Your IP address
- Any analytics, behavioral data, or usage telemetry
- Your Apple ID, email, or any personal identifiers

The keyboard does **not require "Allow Full Access"** for typing. Full Access is optional and only enables on-device haptic feedback (a vibration) when you tap a key. Even with Full Access enabled, the keyboard performs **no network requests**.

## 2. Information stored locally on your device

The App stores the following on your device only:

- Your **theme preference** (e.g., Dark, Habesha, Ocean).
- Your **default keyboard language** preference (Tigrinya, Tigre, or Amharic).
- Your **premium themes purchase status** (whether you've bought the premium themes one-time IAP).

These are kept in iOS App Group preferences (`UserDefaults`) so the keyboard extension and the main app can share them. They never leave your device.

## 3. In-App Purchases

Bela Keyboard offers a one-time **Premium Themes** in-app purchase. Purchases are processed entirely by **Apple** through StoreKit. We do not see, store, or process your payment information. Apple's privacy policy applies to the transaction: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## 4. Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## 5. Third-party services

The current version (v1.0) of Bela Keyboard uses **no third-party SDKs**, no analytics services, and no advertising networks. If we add any in the future (for example, voice-to-text or ads in the host app), this policy will be updated and the change will be summarized at the top of this page.

## 6. Data retention

Because we don't collect or transmit any personal data, there is nothing to retain. The preferences stored locally on your device can be erased at any time by uninstalling the App.

## 7. Your rights

You can:

- Delete the App at any time to erase all locally stored preferences.
- Disable the keyboard in **Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards**.
- Contact us at the email below with any privacy questions.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the "Effective date" at the top and post the change here. Material changes will be highlighted.
